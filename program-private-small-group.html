Individual soccer skills development

Choose a focus for your coaching

Talk through what your player wants to practise with the coaching team. Private and small-group sessions use purposeful ball work, game-like challenges and clear feedback.

Ball control & first touch

Discuss receiving the ball, touch, timing and body shape with your coach.

Awareness & decisions

Work on noticing space, teammates and pressure, then choosing the next action.

Confidence in play

Connect a skill with a game-like situation and use feedback to understand what to try next.

You can share a goal under “Tell us more” in the form, or discuss it when the team replies. Goals are optional; your inquiry starts the conversation about a suitable coach, time and venue.
