Soccer Stars I

Star Soccer Vancouver

This group is for players who are ready for more technical detail while keeping the environment energetic, welcoming, and fun.

Sessions are mixed boys and girls and focus on first touch, passing, receiving, scanning, and confidence in small-sided football situations.
