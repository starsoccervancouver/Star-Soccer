Star Soccer Coach Desk

Coach Desk is Star Soccer’s workspace for approved coaches and staff. Coaches use it to see assigned sessions and rosters, record attendance, and prepare session plans and coaching notes.

Open Coach Desk

Google sign-in and Gmail intake

Coach Desk uses Google sign-in to check a staff member’s email and confirm they have an approved account. The club owner can separately connect the Star Soccer Gmail inbox so Coach Desk can bring registration requests and coaching inquiries into the operations ledger and help reconcile payment notices.
