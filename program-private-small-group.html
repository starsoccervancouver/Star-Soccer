How longer sessions are priced

For 90 minutes, the extra half-hour is discounted by 10%. For 120 minutes, the second hour is discounted by 20%. The first hour remains at its normal rate. The total per-player price is rounded to the nearest $5; discounts do not stack. Other session lengths are quoted individually.

How it works

Submit your inquiry, and our team will help arrange a suitable coach, time and location. This is a request, not a confirmed appointment.

Payment after confirmation

Pay by e-transfer after your coach confirms the session. No payment is required to inquire.
