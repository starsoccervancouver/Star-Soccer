Privacy & your information

Information used to arrange coaching, manage registrations and keep attendance records.

Updated October 29, 2026

What the forms ask for

Registration forms ask for a player’s name, age, experience, a parent or guardian email, and the selected program and dates. Depending on the form, you can also provide a parent name, notes, availability, preferred coach or how you heard about us. Contact forms ask for an email and a message; extra details are optional.

Please have a parent or guardian submit youth registrations. Keep medical information and payment-card details out of the website forms. Contact the team directly if you need to discuss information needed for a player’s participation.

How your information is used

The team uses these details to answer enquiries, check the correct age group and availability, organise coaching, reconcile registrations and payments, and send booking and attendance messages. Coaches use rosters, recorded attendance and coaching notes to prepare and deliver sessions.

Submitting a form does not itself reserve a place. Keep the reference shown after submission and wait for the team’s booking confirmation.

Where information goes

  • Website forms: FormSubmit receives your submission and forwards it to Star Soccer’s Gmail inbox.
  • Registrations and attendance: The team uses private Airtable records for families, players, dated bookings and attendance.
  • Attendance links and Coach Desk: These tools run on Cloudflare. They store attendance responses and coaching information, and connect authorised staff to the relevant records. Staff sign in with approved Google accounts.
  • Public availability: The website reads an aggregate Google Sheets feed showing session dates and counts. That public feed does not include player names or parent contact details.

These services process information as part of providing their functions. Emailing the team directly avoids the website form provider but still uses email. Attendance links are personal to your booking; avoid forwarding them publicly.

Browser storage and website measurement

When you arrive through a tagged campaign link, the website can keep the campaign’s source, medium and name in your current tab’s session storage. The tags can be attached to a submitted form for 24 hours after capture. This helps the team understand how people found a program. These tags do not contain the player details entered into the form.

Some booking pages make a non-personal receipt available in the current tab for up to 24 hours. It contains a reference and public booking details, not a player name or parent email. Cloudflare also provides website delivery and traffic measurement. Links to maps, social media and other services open those providers’ websites.

Questions, corrections and record requests

Email starsoccervancouver@gmail.com to ask what information is held about your family, correct a detail, request deletion, or ask how long a particular record is retained. Include enough information to identify the booking, such as its reference. The team may need to verify that the request comes from the parent, guardian or account holder.

This page describes the current website and coaching systems. Ask the team if you need clarification before submitting information.
