Plan your camp day

Where & who

Kitsilano Turf, Vancouver. Camps are for ages 5–14, with a maximum of 20 players in each morning or afternoon session.

Training takes place across both Monday–Friday weeks, including Christmas Day and New Year’s Day.

Bring along

Turf-appropriate footwear, weather-ready layers and water. Bring a packed lunch if attending both daily sessions; the team supervises the noon–12:30 break.

The coaching team confirms your place and shares arrival details. Contact us about weather or schedule changes.

Register for a weekly package or choose your drop-in days. The coaching team confirms places before payment. All sessions are subject to the 20-player capacity. Capacity is 20 per session; morning and afternoon rosters are separate.

On the field

What players will practise

Skills with purpose

Ball work connects touch, timing and body shape with awareness of space, teammates and pressure.

Practice into play

Game-like challenges give players a chance to use their skills and decisions, with clear coaching feedback along the way.

Our winter camps build on the same coaching approach as weekly training. Activities adapt to the group. Ask the team if you would like to discuss your player’s experience or goals.
