Where & who

Kitsilano Turf, Vancouver. Camps are for ages 5–14, with a maximum of 20 players in each morning or afternoon session.

Training takes place across both Monday–Friday weeks, including Christmas Day and New Year’s Day.

Bring along

Turf-appropriate footwear, weather-ready layers and water. Bring a packed lunch if attending both daily sessions; the team supervises the noon–12:30 break.

The coaching team confirms your place and shares arrival details. Contact us about weather or schedule changes.

Submitting a request does not reserve a place automatically. The coaching team confirms available places before payment. Capacity is 20 per session; morning and afternoon rosters are separate.
