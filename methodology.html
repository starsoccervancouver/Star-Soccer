From practice to play

How a training day unfolds

Sessions build from a welcoming start into technical practice, decisions and game-like challenges. The activity changes with the group; the aim is to connect skills with real football moments.

  1. Arrival and activation

    Get players moving, comfortable and ready to take part.

  2. Ball work and awareness

    Practice touch, timing and body shape, then introduce scanning and choices.

  3. Play, compete and reflect

    Apply skills under realistic pressure and leave with a clear coaching point to work on.

Three connected skills

The TPD method

We develop players who can understand a situation and choose how to respond, not simply repeat an isolated drill.

Technical Intelligence

Learn how, when and why to use the ball effectively in game situations.

Perception

Scan and notice space, teammates, opponents and pressure before the next action.

Decision Making

Choose an action with purpose and build confidence solving problems in the game.

How we coach

Confidence grows through understanding

A welcoming environment

The first minutes matter. Clear cues and a positive introduction help players settle, participate and feel part of the group.

Repetition with a reason

Purposeful ball work builds comfort and technique. We connect each exercise to something players can recognize in a game.

Challenge and individual expression

Competition and realistic pressure give players room to make decisions, try solutions and learn from them.

Clear demonstrations and feedback

Short coaching points and well-timed corrections help players understand what to notice, what improved and what to practice next.
