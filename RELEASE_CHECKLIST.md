## Important boundaries - not a booking database
GitHub Pages serves static files. Browser validation does **not** enforce prices or capacity against crafted POST requests sent directly to the form service. The catalog and checks prevent erroneous selections in the supported website journey, but are not an authorization boundary. Never treat an emailed price as independently verified: staff should reconcile the session ID, dates and amount against the catalog.

A server-side endpoint/database is still required for authoritative catalog validation, durable registration IDs, inventory/capacity reservations, reliable duplicate prevention across devices, and reconciliation of attendance and payment. No unapproved provider, paid service, arbitrary capacity, or cancellation/refund rule has been introduced.

A receipt's reference is generated by this website, not returned as a verified database record by FormSubmit. The UI confirms acceptance under the owner's policy only after the service reports success. It does not prove inbox delivery. No child names, emails or notes are stored in the receipt; the record stays in session storage in the current tab for up to 24 hours. Storage failure leaves the printable confirmation on the form page.

## Maintaining the schedule
`training.js` contains the approved weekday, time, age limits, rates, coaches and locations. `excludedDates` is initially empty because no cancellations were supplied; populate it only for confirmed exclusions. The date calculation skips explicitly excluded dates for a pass. Keep static Programs and Locations content aligned with this catalog; the static test checks IDs and age/location data. Never accept a price query parameter or revive closed camp pages to add a new offering.

Private rates: first hour $60/$45/$40/$35/$30/$25 per player for 1/2/3/4/5/6+ players. For 90 minutes, the extra half-hour is 10% off its standard rate. For 120 minutes, the entire second hour is 20% off its standard rate and the first hour stays full-price; discounts do not stack. Round the whole-session per-player quote once to the nearest $5, then multiply by player count. Two-hour per-player totals are $110/$80/$70/$65/$55/$45 for 1/2/3/4/5/6+ players. Other durations require a coach quote; the form explains this rule explicitly.
