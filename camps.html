Common questions

Camps FAQ

What ages are summer camps for?

Camps run for U4–U14 players across three age groups: Mini Stars Camp (U4–U6), Soccer Stars Camp (U7–U10), and Soccer Stars Camp (U11–U14).

Where are summer camps located?

Camps run at Kitsilano High School Turf and Trimble Park in Vancouver, BC. Both venues are well-maintained turf fields ideal for summer training.

When do summer camps run?

Camps begin June 29, 2026 and run weekday mornings through the end of August. Morning sessions run 9:30 AM–12:00 PM and afternoon sessions 12:30–3:00 PM.

What do players work on during camp?

Technical skill development, first touches, passing, game understanding, scanning, and small-sided game confidence — all in a positive, coach-led environment focused on long-term player growth.

How do I register for summer camps?

Select your age group above to find weekly options, dates, and registration details. You can also contact us directly.

Ready for summer camp?

High-energy summer camps in Vancouver for U4–U14 players at Kitsilano High School Turf and Trimble Park. Skill-focused sessions, experienced coaches, and a summer of real improvement.
