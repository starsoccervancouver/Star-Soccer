Program philosophy

Soccer Stars I Approach

Who it is for

Players who are ready for more technical detail, better habits in possession, and growing confidence in real game situations.

How it feels

Energetic, organized, and skill-based, with plenty of touches and guided decisions throughout the session.

Next step

This age band builds the foundation for more advanced U11-U14 training and stronger football awareness.

Soccer Stars helps players grow technique, awareness, and real confidence in the flow of the game.
