Longer-session discounts and rounding

Prices are in CAD per player. The first 60 minutes are charged at the standard rate. For a 90-minute session, the extra half-hour is 10% off its standard rate. For a two-hour session, the entire second hour is 20% off its standard rate; the first hour is not discounted. Discounts do not stack. The full session price is rounded once to the nearest $5 per player, then multiplied by the player count. Other durations are quoted by the coaching team.
